Privacy PolicyGizlilik Politikası

Effective date: 25 October 2026

This policy explains what information the Bubble Odyssey mobile game ("the game") and the services built into it collect, why, and what choices you have. The game is published by Bubble Odyssey Team ("we", "us"). Questions: mogulkocbali@gmail.com.

The short version

  • You do not need an account. We do not ask for your real name, email, phone number, contacts, photos or location.
  • Your progress (levels, stars, coins, settings) is saved on your device.
  • Signing in with Google Play Games is optional. If you join the in-game leaderboard, we store the leaderboard name you choose with your total stars and completed levels, and other players can see them. You can leave the leaderboard at any time.
  • The game shows ads through Google AdMob and measures how the game is played with Google Analytics for Firebase. These Google services process device identifiers as described below.
  • In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy choices.

1. Information stored on your device

Game progress, coins, boosters, lives, daily-reward and settings data are stored locally on your device. We do not receive this data. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes it.

1a. Leaderboard (optional)

If you sign in with Google Play Games and pick a leaderboard name, we store a leaderboard entry in Google Cloud Firestore (servers in Frankfurt, Germany): the name you chose, your total stars, your number of completed levels, the time of the last update and a Firebase user ID linked to your Play Games account. Your name, stars and level count are shown to every player who opens the leaderboard. Pick a name that does not reveal who you are. You can change the name, or delete the entry and its user ID, in the game: Leaderboard → pencil → Leave the leaderboard. You can also email us to have it deleted. Names that are offensive or impersonate others may be removed.

3. Advertising and your choices

  • EEA, UK, Switzerland: on first launch a consent form (Google's User Messaging Platform) lets you accept or reject personalised ads. If you reject, you may still see non-personalised ads. You can reopen the form in Settings → Privacy choices.
  • Everywhere: you can reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalisation, in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Rewarded videos (for example extra shots) are always optional.

4. Legal bases (EEA/UK)

Personalised advertising relies on your consent. Non-personalised advertising, analytics, fraud prevention and remote configuration rely on our legitimate interest in running, funding and improving a free game. The leaderboard and in-app purchases are processed to provide the features you choose to use.

5. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

6. Retention

Local game data stays on your device until you uninstall the game or clear its data. Analytics data is kept according to the retention period configured in Google Analytics (at most 14 months). Ad-related data is retained by Google according to its own policies. A leaderboard entry is kept until you leave the leaderboard or ask us to delete it.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or Turkey's Law No. 6698 on the Protection of Personal Data, "KVKK"), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your personal data, to withdraw consent, and to complain to a data protection authority. The only profile we hold is your leaderboard entry, if you created one: you can change or delete it in the game, or email us. Other requests are best handled through the controls above or directly with Google; if you need help, email us.

8. Security

Data sent to Google services is transmitted over encrypted connections. Leaderboard entries are stored in Google Cloud Firestore; security rules let each player write only their own entry.

9. Changes

We may update this policy, for example when we add new features or services. The effective date above shows the latest version. Significant changes will also be announced in the game.
