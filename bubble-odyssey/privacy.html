Privacy PolicyGizlilik Politikası

Effective date: 20 October 2026

This policy explains what information the Bubble Odyssey mobile game ("the game") and the services built into it collect, why, and what choices you have. The game is published by Bubble Odyssey Team ("we", "us"). Questions: mogulkocbali@gmail.com.

The short version

  • You do not need an account. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos or location.
  • Your progress (levels, stars, coins, settings) is saved only on your device.
  • The game shows ads through Google AdMob and measures how the game is played with Google Analytics for Firebase. These Google services process device identifiers as described below.
  • In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy choices.

1. Information stored on your device

Game progress, coins, boosters, lives, daily-reward and settings data are stored locally on your device. We do not receive this data. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes it.

2. Information processed by third-party services

ServiceWhat it processesWhy
Google AdMob (including the User Messaging Platform) Advertising ID, IP address, device and app information (model, OS version, language), ad interactions, consent choice Showing ads, measuring them, preventing fraud, and recording your consent choice
Google Analytics for Firebase App instance ID, Advertising ID (where permitted), game events such as level started / won / failed, booster use, device model, OS version, approximate country Understanding how the game is played so we can balance levels and fix problems
Firebase Remote Config App instance ID, app version Adjusting game settings (for example level difficulty) without an update

These services are provided by Google LLC / Google Ireland Ltd. Their processing is governed by Google's terms and policies: Google Privacy Policy, How Google uses information from apps that use its services, Firebase privacy. Data may be processed on servers outside your country, including in the United States.

3. Advertising and your choices

  • EEA, UK, Switzerland: on first launch a consent form (Google's User Messaging Platform) lets you accept or reject personalised ads. If you reject, you may still see non-personalised ads. You can reopen the form in Settings → Privacy choices.
  • Everywhere: you can reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalisation, in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Rewarded videos (for example extra shots) are always optional.

4. Legal bases (EEA/UK)

Personalised advertising relies on your consent. Non-personalised advertising, analytics, fraud prevention and remote configuration rely on our legitimate interest in running, funding and improving a free game.

5. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

6. Retention

Local game data stays on your device until you uninstall the game or clear its data. Analytics data is kept according to the retention period configured in Google Analytics (at most 14 months). Ad-related data is retained by Google according to its own policies.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or Turkey's Law No. 6698 on the Protection of Personal Data, "KVKK"), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your personal data, to withdraw consent, and to complain to a data protection authority. Because we do not hold an account or profile for you, most requests are best handled through the controls above or directly with Google; if you need help, email us and include the app instance ID if you can.

8. Security

Data sent to Google services is transmitted over encrypted connections. We do not run our own servers that store player data.

9. Changes

We may update this policy, for example when we add in-app purchases or new services. The effective date above shows the latest version. Significant changes will also be announced in the game.
