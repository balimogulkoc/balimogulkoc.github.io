Privacy PolicyGizlilik Politikası

Effective date: 8 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Bubble Odyssey mobile game ("the game") and the services built into it collect, why, and what choices you have. The game is published by Velaris Labs ("we", "us"). Questions: velarislabstudio@gmail.com.

The short version

  • You do not need an account. We do not ask for your real name, email, phone number, contacts, photos or location.
  • Your progress (levels, stars, coins, settings) is saved on your device. If you are signed in with Google Play Games, a private copy is also backed up so you can get it back on a new phone.
  • Signing in with Google Play Games is optional. If you join the in-game leaderboard, we store the leaderboard name you choose with your total stars and completed levels, and other players can see them. You can leave the leaderboard at any time.
  • The game shows ads through Google AdMob and measures how the game is played with Google Analytics for Firebase. These Google services process device identifiers as described below.
  • In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy choices.

1. Information stored on your device

Game progress, coins, boosters, lives, daily-reward and settings data are stored locally on your device. If you are not signed in with Google Play Games, we do not receive this data, and uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes it. If you are signed in, a copy is backed up as described in section 1b.

1a. Leaderboard (optional)

If you sign in with Google Play Games and pick a leaderboard name, we store a leaderboard entry in Google Cloud Firestore (servers in Frankfurt, Germany): the name you chose, your total stars, your number of completed levels, the time of the last update and a Firebase user ID linked to your Play Games account. Your name, stars and level count are shown to every player who opens the leaderboard. Pick a name that does not reveal who you are. You can change the name, or delete the entry and its user ID, in the game: Leaderboard → pencil → Leave the leaderboard. You can also email us to have it deleted. Names that are offensive or impersonate others may be removed.

1b. Progress backup (Google Play Games players)

From version 1.0.7, while you are signed in with Google Play Games, the game automatically keeps a copy of its data (level progress, stars, coins, boosters, lives, daily-reward and settings data) and the time of the last update in Google Cloud Firestore (servers in Frankfurt, Germany), under a Firebase user ID linked to your Play Games account. This lets you restore your progress on a new phone. Only you can read the backup; other players never see it. You can delete the backup and stop backups at any time in the game: Settings → Cloud backup. If you have no leaderboard entry, this also deletes the Firebase user ID. Leaving the leaderboard deletes the backup and turns backups off too. Backups stay off on your device until you turn them back on or reinstall the game. You can also email us to have a backup deleted.

3. Advertising and your choices

  • EEA, UK, Switzerland: on first launch a consent form (Google's User Messaging Platform) lets you accept or reject personalised ads. If you reject, you may still see non-personalised ads. You can reopen the form in Settings → Privacy choices.
  • Everywhere: you can reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalisation, in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Rewarded videos (for example extra shots) are always optional.

4. Legal bases (EEA/UK)

Personalised advertising relies on your consent. Non-personalised advertising, analytics, fraud prevention and remote configuration rely on our legitimate interest in running, funding and improving a free game. The leaderboard and in-app purchases are processed to provide the features you choose to use. The progress backup relies on our legitimate interest in keeping your progress safe.

5. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

6. Retention

Local game data stays on your device until you uninstall the game or clear its data. Analytics data is kept according to the retention period configured in Google Analytics (at most 14 months). Ad-related data is retained by Google according to its own policies. A leaderboard entry is kept until you leave the leaderboard or ask us to delete it. A progress backup is kept until you turn backups off, leave the leaderboard or ask us to delete it, and is replaced each time the game backs up.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or Turkey's Law No. 6698 on the Protection of Personal Data, "KVKK"), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your personal data, to withdraw consent, and to complain to a data protection authority. The only data we hold about you is your progress backup, if you signed in with Play Games, and your leaderboard entry, if you created one: you can change or delete the entry and delete the backup in the game, or email us about either. Other requests are best handled through the controls above or directly with Google; if you need help, email us.

8. Security

Data sent to Google services is transmitted over encrypted connections. Leaderboard entries and progress backups are stored in Google Cloud Firestore; security rules let each player write only their own entry and read only their own backup.

9. Changes

We may update this policy, for example when we add new features or services. The effective date above shows the latest version. Significant changes will also be announced in the game.

1. Cihazında saklanan bilgiler

Oyun ilerlemesi, altınlar, güçlendiriciler, canlar, günlük ödül ve ayar bilgileri cihazında saklanır. Google Play Games ile giriş yapmadıysan bu bilgiler bize gönderilmez; oyunu kaldırmak veya verilerini temizlemek onları siler. Giriş yaptıysan bir kopyası 1b bölümünde anlatıldığı gibi yedeklenir.

1a. Sıralama (isteğe bağlı)

Google Play Games ile giriş yapıp bir sıralama adı seçersen Google Cloud Firestore'da (sunucular Frankfurt, Almanya) bir sıralama kaydı tutarız: seçtiğin ad, toplam yıldızın, tamamladığın seviye sayısı, son güncelleme zamanı ve Play Games hesabına bağlı bir Firebase kullanıcı kimliği. Adın, yıldızların ve seviye sayın sıralamayı açan her oyuncuya gösterilir. Kim olduğunu belli etmeyen bir ad seç. Adını oyunda değiştirebilir, kaydını ve kullanıcı kimliğini silebilirsin: Sıralama → kalem → Sıralamadan çık. Silinmesi için bize e-posta da gönderebilirsin. Hakaret içeren veya başkasını taklit eden adlar kaldırılabilir.

1b. İlerleme yedeği (Google Play Games oyuncuları)

1.0.7 sürümünden itibaren, Google Play Games ile girişliyken oyun, verilerinin bir kopyasını (seviye ilerlemesi, yıldızlar, altınlar, güçlendiriciler, canlar, günlük ödül ve ayar bilgileri) ve son güncelleme zamanını, Play Games hesabına bağlı bir Firebase kullanıcı kimliğiyle Google Cloud Firestore'da (sunucular Frankfurt, Almanya) otomatik olarak saklar. Böylece ilerlemeni yeni telefonda geri alabilirsin. Yedeği yalnızca sen okuyabilirsin, diğer oyuncular göremez. Yedeği silip yedeklemeyi istediğin zaman oyundan durdurabilirsin: Ayarlar → Bulut yedeği. Sıralama kaydın yoksa bu işlem Firebase kullanıcı kimliğini de siler. Sıralamadan çıkmak da yedeği siler ve yedeklemeyi kapatır. Yedekleme, yeniden açana veya oyunu yeniden kurana kadar cihazında kapalı kalır. Yedeğin silinmesi için bize e-posta da gönderebilirsin.
